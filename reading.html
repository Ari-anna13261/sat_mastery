Reading & Writing

Main Idea

The Main Idea is what the author wants you to understand after reading the passage.

Tip

Don't focus on one sentence. Look for the overall message.

Inference

Inference questions ask what is implied—not directly stated.

Tip

Only choose answers supported by the passage.

Vocabulary in Context

SAT vocabulary questions depend on context, not dictionary definitions.

Tip
